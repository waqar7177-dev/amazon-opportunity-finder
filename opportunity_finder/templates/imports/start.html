How rows are checked

  • Only ASIN, Title or Amazon URL is required. Missing values are never guessed — those products show as Incomplete Data.
  • Every row is validated like the product form. Rows with problems are listed with the reason and are not imported.
  • Rows matching products you already have: choose to skip, update or create duplicates in the preview.
  • The import saves in one step — if anything fails, nothing is saved.
